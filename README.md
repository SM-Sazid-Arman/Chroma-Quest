Chroma Quest is a high-energy arcade game designed to test your speed and focus. You can play it directly in your browser at: [https://sm-sazid-arman.github.io/Chroma-Quest/](https://sm-sazid-arman.github.io/Chroma-Quest/)

### What’s the Goal?
The objective is simple: navigate a randomized maze to collect coins of a specific target color. You must pick them up in a strict numerical sequence from 1 to 10 before the 60-second timer hits zero. Picking the wrong color or the wrong number results in a score penalty and a screen-shake effect. If you’re running low on time, grab the white ⏱ coins to add 5 seconds to your clock.

---

### How to Play
* **On Desktop:** Use your **Arrow keys** or **W, A, S, and D** to move through the grid.
* **On Mobile:** Simply **swipe** in the direction you want to move.
* **Pause & Mute:** Tap the buttons at the top of the screen to pause the action or toggle the synthesized sound effects.

---

### Key Features
* **Unique Maps:** Every round generates a new, solvable layout, so you will never play the same maze twice.
* **Smart Minimap:** A map in the corner reveals the area as you explore and "remembers" coin locations for a few seconds after you leave their vicinity.
* **No Loading Times:** All sound effects are synthesized in real-time by the browser, so there are no heavy audio files to download.
* **Performance Focused:** The entire game is built into a single, lightweight file, making it fast and responsive on both phones and PCs.
